# TidySheetStudioFinds — Messaging Playbook

Five niches, one segment: solo/small service operators who are too small for the SaaS and too grown for the notebook. Below is exactly what to say, in their words, and where to say it.

**2) WORDS THAT SELL**

**4) LISTING UPGRADE**

- Improved line: *"Every door code, gate code, feeding portion, med schedule and emergency vet — one Google Sheet, paid once. No $30/month software to learn for a book of a few clients."*

**5) WHERE TO SHOW UP**

## THE THREE MESSAGES THAT WORK EVERYWHERE

**1. "Pay once. No monthly. No app that gets more expensive as you grow."**
Every niche has the same villain — a subscription that scales against the little guy: a *"pretty costly monthly fee especially for a small start-up with very few clients"* (pet sitting), *"went from $39 a month to $200"* (grooming), Jobber's jump *"from $49 to $119"* the day you add a person (cleaning), Skimmer's per-pool fee *"from $1 to $2"* (pool), *"~$150/month"* breeder apps. The competitor Etsy sheet already sells on *"no subscriptions or special software needed."* Lead every listing with the one-time price against the named monthly villain — it is the wedge in all five.

**2. "Everything in one place you can actually find — stop scrolling and digging."**
The shared daily reality is scattered chaos: *"a patchwork of texts, calls, and sticky notes,"* *"47 messages up in the chat,"* the sticky note that said *"Call Debbie – Thursday,"* readings *"scribbled in a notebook that gets lost,"* *"twenty minutes of scrolling to find out who paid what."* They aren't buying features — they're buying **retrieval**. Promise the two-tap lookup: one row, one tab, findable at the door / on the phone / mid-call.

**3. "More organized than your notebook — simpler than the software you'll never finish setting up."**
They reject both extremes in the same breath: the SaaS is *"complicated,"* a *"learning curve,"* *"6–7 screens just to get some info,"* while their own DIY sheet is *"a very plain spreadsheet I created myself… gives me headaches,"* *"held together with duct tape and memory."* The winning position is the Goldilocks middle — **pre-built, pretty, and made for their exact records** (door codes, blade lengths, chemical dosing, daily grams), with fields locked so they can't break it. "Done-for-you and made for your job, without the software you'll never finish setting up" converts across all five.
