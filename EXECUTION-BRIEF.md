## 6. FEEDBACK LOOP

### Measure in weeks 1–2 (dashboard: one spreadsheet, updated daily)
- **Upwork:** proposals sent, viewed-by-client rate, reply rate, calls booked. *The tell:* viewed-rate <30% = bad targeting/first line; viewed-but-no-reply = bad Loom/pitch; healthy = >8% reply.
- **Fiverr:** gig impressions, clicks, inquiries, orders (all free in seller dashboard). *The tell:* impressions near zero by day 10 = search volume is the mirage the validation feared — no amount of copy fixes that.
- **Niche vitality:** count new "claude setup/training" job posts per week on Upwork. Falling count = window closing early.

### Iterate-vs-kill rule (a number and a date)
- **Iterate checkpoint — Day 14 (July 16):** if Upwork reply rate <5% over ≥25 proposals, rewrite the proposal + re-record the Loom + re-title the gig once. One iteration, not endless tuning.
- **Kill/pivot rule — Day 21 (July 23):** if by end of day 21 you have **fewer than 1 paid order/job total AND fewer than 2 genuine buyer conversations across both platforms**, kill the Sprint as primary and pivot the *same* Upwork profile to the **n8n/Make Workflow Fixer** (score-8 runner-up): 1 day to add 2–3 demo workflows and re-title, remaining Connects carry over, and 811 verified open jobs are waiting. This is the pre-planned fallback, not a new decision.
- **Scale rule — Day 30:** ≥3 orders and ≥1 five-star review → raise prices per Section 3, launch gig #2, hold course.

### Feeding the next scan cycle
Log every buyer interaction verbatim: what they asked for that wasn't in your tiers (repeated asks = next productized offer, e.g., "can you do this monthly?" = the retainer/maintenance product — the actual semi-automated recurring asset you want), which vertical converts best (that vertical's recipe pack becomes a standalone $29–49 digital template product once you have reviews to launch it against — the true product play this service is the wedge into), and which channel produced each dollar. Week 8's scan cycle should start from that log, not from cold research: the highest-value output of this 60-day run isn't the $1,000 — it's verified, paid-for evidence of what SMBs actually buy in the Claude ecosystem, which is exactly what killed your product ideas last cycle: you didn't have it.
