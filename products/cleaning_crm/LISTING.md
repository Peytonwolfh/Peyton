**Every key code, gate code and alarm code — in one place, on your phone, at
the door.** Not on a sticky note in the van. Not in last month's notebook. Not
in a text you have to scroll back three weeks to find.

Cleaning is a business built on trust and access, and no free calendar app has
anywhere to put "side gate — key under the blue planter", "alarm 2255 then #",
"lockbox 5127, code word Willow", or "CAUTION: guard dog, owner must crate
first." This spreadsheet was built for house cleaners and maid services —
those fields aren't an afterthought, they're the whole point. And unlike
field-service apps like Jobber and ZenMaid at $39–49/MONTH, you pay once and
it's yours forever.

**WHY NOT JUST A NOTEBOOK?** Because the notebook works right up until it
doesn't — the day it's in the other car, the day a code changes and three
pages disagree, the day you hire your first helper and can't hand them "what's
in your head." One misremembered alarm code is a callout fee and an awkward
phone call. This keeps the whole book in your pocket, searchable, and backed
up.

**WHAT'S INSIDE — 7 linked tabs**

🧽 CLIENTS — the tab that pays for itself. Per client: phone, email, service
address, and PROPERTY ACCESS the way you actually need it — Entry / Key
Location, Lockbox Code, Alarm Code and Garage Code each in their own column.
Plus pets on site, who supplies the products (client-supplied vs. you-supply)
and any allergies or fragrance rules, preferred contact, and an Active /
Inactive / Waitlist status. Type CAUTION anywhere in Pets on Site or Notes and
the whole row turns red — a loose dog or a finicky alarm is the last thing you
want to forget on the doorstep.

🧽 JOB DETAILS — one row per home: bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, your
room-by-room scope checklist, a recurring-cadence dropdown (Weekly / Biweekly /
Monthly / One-off / Deep Clean), and both a flat rate and an hourly rate for
that home. The cadence you set here drives the reminders on your schedule.

🧽 SCHEDULE — pick the client and service from dropdowns and the rate fills
itself in from your price list. Today's cleans glow blue. Finished-but-unpaid
cleans turn red until you're paid. And the NEXT CLEAN DUE date calculates
itself from each home's cadence — then turns amber when it's time to book the
next visit. That column alone keeps recurring clients from quietly slipping
away.

🧽 DASHBOARD — updates itself: active clients, cleans this month, cleans this
week, cleans today, unpaid balance, revenue this month, and your top clients
ranked by revenue. Open it each morning and you know your day and your money.

🧽 INVOICES & PAYMENTS — pick a client and a date range; the invoice total and
balance due add themselves up from your completed cleans. Status colors: Paid
is green, Sent is amber, Overdue is red.

🧽 SERVICES & RATES — your editable price list (Standard Clean, Deep Clean,
Move-Out / Move-In, your Recurring rate, your hourly rate, and add-ons like
inside fridge, inside oven, interior windows, cabinets and laundry). Change a
price once and every new clean uses it.

🧽 START HERE — plain-English setup guide written for people who have never
touched a spreadsheet formula. Sample data is pre-loaded (a weekly hardwood
house, a biweekly apartment, a monthly deep clean with a guard dog, a move-out
quote) so you can see how everything works — including the red CAUTION row and
the amber "due" reminders — then delete it in one click.

**WORKS IN GOOGLE SHEETS *AND* EXCEL**

Built with 100% standard formulas — no macros, no add-ons, no subscription.
Upload the file to Google Drive and it just works (a free Google account is
enough), or open it straight in Microsoft Excel. Use it on your phone with the
free Google Sheets app right on the doorstep. (It holds access codes, so keep
the file private — the guide shows you how.)
