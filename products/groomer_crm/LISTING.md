**"What did we do last time?" — answered before the dog is on the table.**
Every cut, every blade length, every coat quirk, every bite risk — in ONE
place, on your phone, at the tub.

Square Appointments and Fresha were built for hair salons. They have nowhere
to put "#4F body, leave the ears long", "NEVER shave the husky — owner
confirmed twice", or "CAUTION: nippy when paws are handled." This spreadsheet
was built for dog groomers — those fields aren't an afterthought, they're the
whole point. And unlike grooming software at $49–79/MONTH, you pay once and
it's yours forever.

**WHAT'S INSIDE — 7 linked tabs**

🐩 PETS — the tab that pays for itself. Per dog: breed, coat type dropdown
(Double Coat / Curly / Wire / Silky / Smooth), LAST CUT & STYLE NOTES (exactly
what the owner asked for), the blade & comb lengths you used, shampoo &
products, skin conditions / allergies, matting trouble spots, and ear / nail /
gland preferences. Type the word CAUTION anywhere in the behavior notes and
the entire row turns red — nobody on your table gets surprised by a bite risk.

🐩 APPOINTMENTS — pick the client and service from dropdowns and the rate
fills itself in from your price list. Today's appointments glow. Finished but
unpaid grooms turn red until you're paid. Type the rebook interval in weeks
(4–8 for most dogs) and the NEXT GROOM DUE date calculates itself — then turns
amber when it's time to call the client and rebook. That column alone fills
slow weeks.

🐩 DASHBOARD — updates itself: active clients, dogs groomed this month,
appointments this week, appointments today, unpaid balance, revenue this
month, and your top clients ranked by revenue. Open it each morning and you
know your day.

🐩 CLIENTS — owner contact info, preferred contact method (text / call /
email), emergency contact, vet clinic + phone, and an Active / Inactive /
Waitlist status.

🐩 INVOICES & PAYMENTS — pick a client and a date range; the invoice total and
balance due add themselves up from your appointments. Status colors: Paid is
green, Sent is amber, Overdue is red.

🐩 SERVICES & RATES — your editable price list (Full Groom Small / Medium /
Large, Bath & Brush, Nail Trim, De-shed, Puppy Intro). Change a price once,
every new appointment uses it.

🐩 START HERE — plain-English setup guide written for people who have never
touched a spreadsheet formula. Sample data is pre-loaded (a matted doodle, a
never-shave husky, a nippy chihuahua...) so you can see how everything works,
then delete it in one click.

**WORKS IN GOOGLE SHEETS *AND* EXCEL**

Built with 100% standard formulas — no macros, no add-ons, no subscription.
Upload the file to Google Drive and it just works (free Google account is
enough), or open it straight in Microsoft Excel. Use it on your phone with
the free Google Sheets app right at the grooming table.

⬇ You receive one .xlsx file immediately after purchase — nothing is shipped.
⬇ Use it for your business forever. Lifetime access, free to reuse every year.
⬇ This is a digital product; because of its nature, no refunds — but message
