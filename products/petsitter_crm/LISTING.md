**Every door code, gate code, alarm instruction, key location, vet number and
medication schedule — in ONE place, on your phone, at the client's front door.**

Square, Fresha and the other free booking apps were built for salons. They
have nowhere to put "garage keypad 4821#", "thyroid pill at 8am hidden in a
treat", or "CAUTION: reactive to bikes." This spreadsheet was built for pet
sitters and dog walkers — those fields aren't an afterthought, they're the
whole point.

**WHAT'S INSIDE — 7 linked tabs**

🏠 DASHBOARD — updates itself: active clients, pets under care, visits this
week, visits today, unpaid balance, revenue this month, and your top clients
ranked by revenue. Open it each morning and you know your day.

🏠 CLIENTS — owner contact info PLUS the fields sitters actually need: door /
gate codes, alarm instructions, key location, emergency contact, vet clinic +
phone, preferred contact method, and an Active / Inactive / Waitlist status.

🏠 PETS — feeding instructions, medications + schedule, walk routine, grooming
notes, and behavior warnings. Type the word CAUTION anywhere in the behavior
notes and the entire row turns red — impossible to miss before a visit.

🏠 VISIT SCHEDULE — pick the client and service from dropdowns and the rate
fills itself in from your price list. Today's visits glow green. Completed
but unpaid visits turn red until you're paid. Recurring field for weekly /
biweekly regulars.

🏠 INVOICES & PAYMENTS — pick a client and a date range; the invoice total and
the balance due add themselves up from your schedule. Status colors: Paid is
green, Sent is amber, Overdue is red.

🏠 SERVICES & RATES — your editable price list (walks, drop-ins, overnights,
boarding). Change a price once, every new visit uses it.

🏠 START HERE — plain-English setup guide written for people who have never
touched a spreadsheet formula. Sample data is pre-loaded so you can see how
everything works, then delete it in one click.

**WORKS IN GOOGLE SHEETS *AND* EXCEL**

Built with 100% standard formulas — no macros, no add-ons, no subscription.
Upload the file to Google Drive and it just works (free Google account is
enough), or open it straight in Microsoft Excel. Use it on your phone with
the free Google Sheets app while you're standing on the client's porch.
